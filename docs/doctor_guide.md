# Clinical Operations & EMR Guide — Rehab Swat CMS

Welcome to the clinical portal of Rehab Swat CMS. This manual outlines clinical charting, exercise prescription, progress tracking, and outcome measure documentation inside our integrated electronic medical record (EMR).

---

## 1. Writing a SOAP Note
All patient consults must be documented using the standard SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) format. 

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **Patients** dashboard and search for your active patient.
2. Select the patient, click the **"EMR & SOAP Notes"** tab, and click **"New SOAP Note"**.
3. Fill in the standardized clinical fields:
   - **Subjective (S)**: Record patient’s verbal complaints, onset details, and pain level (e.g., `Patient reports moderate lower back stiffness, radiating to left gluteal region since lifting heavy boxes 3 days ago. Pain is 6/10.`).
   - **Objective (O)**: Enter physical exam findings, range of motion (ROM), manual muscle tests, and palpation results (e.g., `Lumbar flexion restricted to 45°. Lasegue's sign (SLR) positive on left at 50°. Left piriformis hypertonicity.`).
   - **Assessment (A)**: Outline your clinical diagnosis, progress updates, and rehabilitation potential (e.g., `L4-L5 disc protrusion with mild radiculopathy. Stage: Acute. Rehab potential: Excellent.`).
   - **Plan (P)**: Detail home plans, upcoming sessions, and treatment frequency (e.g., `Start lumbar decompression protocols, McKenzie extension exercises, core stabilization. 3x weekly for 4 weeks.`).
4. Click **"Save Draft"** if you need to return to it later.
5. Click **"Finalize & Lock Note"** once the entry is complete.
   - *CRITICAL*: Once locked, a note becomes legally binding and cannot be edited. A lock icon will appear indicating permanent status.

## 2. Creating a Treatment Plan
A comprehensive treatment plan defines goals, timelines, and milestones.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Under the patient profile, select the **"Treatment Plans"** tab.
2. Click **"Create Treatment Plan"**.
3. Specify the following parameters:
   - **Primary Goal**: The target functional improvement (e.g., `Increase lumbar extension ROM to full and reduce pain score to <2/10.`).
   - **Frequency & Duration**: Set the timeframe (e.g., `3 sessions per week` for `4 weeks`).
   - **Functional Milestones**: Add checkable milestones (e.g., `[ ] Independent SLR to 70° without radiculopathy`, `[ ] Restore pain-free normal gait`).
4. Click **"Activate Plan"**.

---

## 3. Prescribing Exercises
The exercise builder allows you to prescribe custom home programs.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Within an active treatment plan, scroll to the **"Exercise Prescriptions"** section.
2. Click **"Add Exercise Protocol"**.
3. Complete the form:
   - **Exercise Name**: Select or type (e.g., `McKenzie Lumbar Extension`).
   - **Sets & Reps**: Input instructions (e.g., `3 sets` of `10 repetitions`).
   - **Hold Time**: Set the time (e.g., `10-second hold`).
   - **Resistance**: Specify equipment needed (e.g., `Bodyweight` or `Yellow Theraband`).
   - **Frequency**: Clarify daily count (e.g., `2 times daily`).
4. Click **"Prescribe to Patient"**. This feeds directly into the patient's mobile catalog portal.

---

## 4. Logging a Session
As patients complete their in-clinic visits, you must log the actual exercise progress and pain feedback.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to the patient's **Treatment Plans** screen and click **"Log Daily Session"**.
2. Record the following:
   - **Sets Completed**: Note actual exercise execution details.
   - **Pre-Session Pain (VAS)**: Record the patient's pain level before treatment on a slider scale from `0` to `10`.
   - **Post-Session Pain (VAS)**: Record their pain level after the treatment.
   - **Therapist Notes**: Note tolerances, changes in resistance, or active symptoms.
3. Click **"Save Session Log"**. The CMS will immediately map these data points onto a visual **Pain Progress Chart** rendering chronological trends.

---

## 5. Writing a Prescription
If pharmacological support is needed, issue a clinical prescription.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. On the patient profile, select the **"Prescriptions"** tab.
2. Click **"New Prescription"**.
3. Under **Medications**, click **"+ Add Medication"**:
   - **Drug Name**: (e.g., `Tab. Celecoxib 200mg`).
   - **Dosage**: (e.g., `1 tablet`).
   - **Frequency**: (e.g., `Once daily after meals`).
   - **Duration**: (e.g., `5 days`).
4. Select clinical instructions from standard dropdowns or write freeform.
5. Click **"Generate & Sign Prescription"**.

---

## 6. Reviewing Outcome Measures
We measure progress using scientific self-reported scales (e.g., Oswestry Disability Index - ODI, Neck Disability Index - NDI, Patient-Specific Functional Scale - PSFS).

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click the **"Outcome Measures"** tab on the patient's profile.
2. Review the chronological records in the list.
3. Click on the visual **"Disability Progress Graph"** to inspect comparative trends of score decreases over time (lower scores indicate reduced clinical impairment).
