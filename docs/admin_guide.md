# Administration & Configuration Manual — Rehab Swat CMS

Welcome to the Administrator portal of Rehab Swat CMS. Use this manual to configure system settings, coordinate clinic capacity, monitor inventory, check financial analytics, manage service catalogs, and execute database backups.

---

## 1. Adding a New Staff Member
Only users with the `SUPER_ADMIN` role can register new staff accounts and grant permissions.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to the **Staff Management** screen via the sidebar.
2. Click the **"+ Register Staff"** button.
3. Complete the registration form:
   - **Full Name**: (e.g., `Dr. Aisha Khan`).
   - **Email Address**: Senders use this as their login identifier.
   - **Password**: Assign a temporary secure password.
   - **Role**: Select from the dropdown:
     - `SUPER_ADMIN` (Full administrative power)
     - `DOCTOR` (Accesses clinical SOAP, plans, and outcomes)
     - `RECEPTIONIST` (Registers patients, schedules, and generates bills)
     - `BILLING` (Generates invoices and processes payments)
     - `INTERN` (Read-only observation capabilities)
4. Click **"Save Staff Account"**.
5. *Deactivation*: To suspend an account, locate the staff member on the dashboard, click **"Deactivate Account"**, and confirm. The status immediately updates to `INACTIVE`, blocking all system login attempts.

## 2. Setting Doctor Availability
Scheduling constraints prevent patients from booking appointments when the doctor is unavailable.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. On the **Staff Management** screen, select the **Doctor Availability** tab or locate the doctor on the roster.
2. Click **"Configure Availability"**.
3. Choose the weekdays they work (Monday through Friday) and define hours:
   - **Start Time**: Set the time (e.g., `09:00 AM`).
   - **End Time**: Set the time (e.g., `05:00 PM`).
   - **Slot Duration**: Select the standard block size (e.g., `30 mins` or `60 mins`).
4. Click **"Apply Schedule"**. Any scheduling attempts outside of these weekly windows will block receptionists from booking.

## 3. Managing Inventory & Supplies
Track consumables, hot packs, splints, and thera-bands to prevent run-outs.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **Inventory** screen from the sidebar.
2. Review the stock list. Items under minimum thresholds display a red badge: `"Low Stock Alert"`.
3. To add a new stock item, click **"Add Stock Item"**:
   - **Item Name**: (e.g., `Rigid Strapping Tape`).
   - **Category**: (e.g., `Consumables` or `Durable Equipment`).
   - **Current Quantity**: (e.g., `100`).
   - **Minimum Stock Threshold**: (e.g., `20` — *triggers alert when count falls below this*).
   - **Supplier Details**: (e.g., `Swat Medical Supplies Ltd`).
4. To record restocks, click **"Restock Item"** on the target line, enter the quantity added, and hit **"Submit"**.
5. To record item usage, click **"Record Usage"**, input the quantity used, and assign it to a staff log.

## 4. Running Clinic Reports & Analytics
Monitor clinic revenue, appointment volumes, patient demographics, and staff productivity.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **Reports & Analytics** screen.
2. Utilize the filters to select a date range (e.g., `01 May 2026` to `21 May 2026`).
3. Review the analytics modules:
   - **Revenue Chart**: A line graph illustrating weekly and monthly earnings in PKR.
   - **Appointment Volume Chart**: A bar graph showing completed versus cancelled visits.
   - **Patient Demographics**: Pie charts displaying age brackets and gender splits.
   - **Doctor Productivity Table**: Tracks the number of sessions, patients seen, and total billing revenue generated by each practitioner.

## 5. Managing the Service Catalog
The Service Catalog controls what items can be added to patient bills.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **Settings** screen and select the **Service Catalog** tab.
2. To create a new service, click **"Create Service"**:
   - **Service Name**: (e.g., `Manual Spinal Decompression`).
   - **Description**: Detail the clinical focus.
   - **Price (PKR)**: (e.g., type `4500` for `₨ 4,500`).
3. Click **"Save Service"**.
4. To modify pricing, click the **"Edit"** pencil icon next to the service, enter the new price, and save.

---

## 6. Triggering a Database Backup
While automatic backups are scheduled at 2:00 AM, you can trigger a backup instantly.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **Settings** screen and select the **System Backups** tab.
2. Click **"Trigger Manual Backup"**.
3. The system executes the underlying `backup.sh` command line synchronously.
4. The dashboard displays the status: `"Backup successfully generated and replicated to Backblaze B2 bucket: rehabswat-backups"`.
5. Review the backup logs in `/var/log/rehabswat_backup.log` if any issues arise.
